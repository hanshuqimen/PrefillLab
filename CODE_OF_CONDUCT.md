# Code of conduct

PrefillLab welcomes contributors of all backgrounds. Be respectful, discuss technical ideas with evidence, and assume good intent while honoring personal boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and unwanted sexual attention are unacceptable. Do not publish another person's private information. Critique work constructively and allow maintainers to keep discussions focused.

Report concerns privately through the maintainer contact shown on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Maintainers may remove harmful content, limit participation, or ban accounts after considering context and impact. Reports will be handled with discretion. This policy applies to project issues, reviews, discussions, and project-related public interactions.
